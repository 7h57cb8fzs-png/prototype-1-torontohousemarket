    ABOUT YOUR REPORT

    Understand what your home could be worth.

    Get a clearer view of your Toronto or GTA home’s position in the market. THM brings available property records, comparable sales and current competition together in a private seller report.

    Created by Alireza Golestan and Mehrdad Golestan, Sales Representatives with CENTURY 21 Leading Edge Realty Inc., Brokerage.

    Planning your next purchase? Explore the buyer report tool.

    Can I check a home that is not listed for sale?

    Yes. Start with your home’s address, including the unit for a condo. The report reviews available property history and market evidence. Some homes have limited records; the team can help confirm missing details.

    What goes into the home value estimate?

    The report considers available comparable sales, home type, size and location, with current asking prices shown as competition. A price range is provided when the evidence supports one. Your own target price does not determine the estimate.

    Does requesting a report commit me to selling?

    No. There is no obligation to list. You must own the home or have the owner’s permission. The report is preliminary guidance, not an appraisal or a guaranteed sale price; discuss the evidence and your next steps with the team.
