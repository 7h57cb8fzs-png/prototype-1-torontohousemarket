    How comparisons work

    Exact MLS community and home type. Size leads the match; bedroom layout and verified lot size refine it. Parking and bathrooms are shown as differences.

    Within 5% of the matched median: in line. More than 5% below or above: lower or higher. Gaps over 25% need review. At least 3 distinct matching listings are required.

    These are sellers’ asking prices. Your private report checks what similar homes actually sold for.
