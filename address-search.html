Privacy & terms · September 15, 2026

Optional address suggestions

When Google Maps suggestions are enabled, the street address you type is sent to Google Places to suggest matching addresses. Selecting a suggestion requests its street and city details. You can also enter your address yourself. Your name, phone number and email are not sent to Google by the address suggestion feature.

Your property request

The address you confirm is used to find the property and prepare the report you request. Contact details and home details submitted with a report request are used to prepare and deliver the report and follow up as described on the form. The website uses service providers for hosting, property data, report preparation and email delivery.

Google Maps terms

Use of Google Maps address suggestions is subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Suggestions help you enter an address; they do not confirm ownership, MLS availability or market value. Please confirm the property and condo unit before requesting a report.
